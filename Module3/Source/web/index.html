Pusaka3D — Textured Temple, real-time (Module 3)

The SAME data/pusaka_model.obj + data/relief.ppm that raster3d.py and raytrace.py render from scratch. Three.js does the MVP stack, the w-divide, the z-buffer, and perspective-correct texturing on the GPU. Drag to orbit.

Export at the same camera as your NumPy render, then python compare.py out/temple_raster.ppm web/temple_three.ppm — expect ~30 dB with the residual on anti-aliased silhouette edges, not in the interior (a transform bug would shift a whole region).
